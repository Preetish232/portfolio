About

Code that looks like it cares.

I'm a Computer Science student at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur. I build chatbots, trading bots and encryption tools in Python, and I lead-design a college magazine in my spare time. Responsible, orderly, and happiest when the logic and the layout both line up.

I'm a Nagpur kid through and through: Sandipnani School first, then Classes 11 and 12 at Modern School, and now college at SIT Nagpur. I speak English, Hindi and Sindhi at home and around town, and I learn best by building things that actually run.

I live in two worlds. In one I write Python: chatbots that talk to customers, a trading bot with real risk rules, an encryption tool that takes key management seriously. In the other I'm the lead designer of our college magazine, choosing type, layout and the look of every edition, and running its social media. Being on the promotion team for college events taught me to plan campaigns, write copy and work with a team to a deadline.

Right now my focus is AI/ML. I'm building a hybrid CNN and Transformer model to restore degraded forensic images, and I'm studying neural networks with PyTorch, NumPy and Pandas along the way. After five weeks of live production work at MOTS Technologies, I know I like shipping things that real people use.
